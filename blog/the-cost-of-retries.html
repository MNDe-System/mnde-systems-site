The cost of retries

How small retry loops become large failures.

A retry is often treated as safe by default. It rarely is. Each retry adds more pressure to the same failing path.

What begins as a small recovery mechanism can become queue growth, duplicate work, extra spend, and user visible instability.

The right place to control retries is before execution, where count, cost, and scope can be evaluated as part of the decision itself.

MNDe refuses requests that have already exceeded policy. That keeps a bounded failure from turning into an expanding one.
